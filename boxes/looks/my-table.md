# My side of the table (the GM's)

In the maintainer's words, 2026-11-22: "I'm a bit old school, I have my
screen, but I have my three ring behind it. So I take pages out of it and
put them either in the screen or on the table for ref. I have a dice box
pretty much center center. and my screen is standard three panel."

Laid out:

- **The screen**, a standard three-panel GM screen, in front of the GM.
- **The binder**, a three-ring binder, behind the screen.
- **Pages** come out of the binder and go to one of two places: clipped
  into the screen (the GM's reference, unseen by the table) or laid on the
  table (reference everyone can see).
- **The dice box**, centre of the table, centre of the view.

How the table sees it (2026-11-22): "The GM would share from their device to
others." One device is the GM's: the screen, the binder and every page. What
the GM puts on the table goes from that device to the others'.

The screen's three panels (2026-11-22): "On the left, I usually have my
quick ref for anything from the bestiary or upcoming loot and planned
battles. In my binder, I have my planned story, on the right is players,
and in the center is active combat. So I guess my binder and the active
can all go in the center panel."

- **Left:** quick reference: the bestiary, upcoming loot, planned battles.
- **Centre:** what's happening now: the binder's planned story, and active
  combat when there is one.
- **Right:** the players.

A quick lookup (2026-11-22): "We also should keep a quick lookup for all
the other things that might turn up, the rules, the books, being able to
ask them on demand." Whatever comes up at the table, asked then and there:
the binder, the rules, the rulebooks.

Where the lookup sits (2026-11-22): "I think it should live in the left,
that comes into the center when called." The lookup is part of the left
panel's quick reference; an answer, called, moves into the centre panel.

Pictures the table makes (2026-11-22): "Since we already have the
tesseract in, that should really be included, because I was just thinking
about images for the creatures, and how a kid could draw their own, with
their character, and put it in." A creature's picture in the bestiary; a
kid's own drawing of their character, or of a creature, brought to the
table and put in.
