# the store, asked what it is

A stack over the SAFE App Store as it was at one commit, its NASA archive
left out (`apps/nasa-archive`: 293 MB, 2,394 of its 4,362 files, a corpus
the rest of the store doesn't need to be read by). Each want is written
above the step that answers it, as a GM's wants are in `box/gm-stack.md`:
they are proposals, in the words the store's own pages use, there to cut.
`rebuild.sh` lays the box and runs it.

## what the store is

Want 1: "A store here is not a shop. What is it?" The map of the house
says provision-house; every passage that says so, ranked.
- all-passages -- --box {box} --top 5 "store provision house"

Want 2: "What does a build have to meet to be promoted?" The bar, as the
law and the docs write it.
- all-passages -- --box {box} --top 5 "promotion bar manifest tests"

Want 3: "Why can't the maker promote their own build?" Proposing and
ratifying never rest in the same hand.
- all-passages -- --box {box} --top 5 "verified_by author promote"

## what it already knew

Want 4: "When did the house first say it already knew?" The day four things
were built that already existed, and the first line that names it.
- first-mention -- --box {box} "house already knew"

Want 5: "What is conflict_scan, really?" Before anything else leans on it:
the recon of Jeles says what it reads, and where.
- all-passages -- --box {box} --top 5 "conflict_scan refutes"

Want 6: "Where did Die-Namic come from?" The first line that names it, and
the playground it was lifted out of.
- first-mention -- --box {box} "die-namic"

## what waits

when its record was last checked.
- box-health -- --box {box}
