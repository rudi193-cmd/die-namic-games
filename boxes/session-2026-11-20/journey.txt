Aetheris — The Waking Tide
The supercontinent is coming apart under a sky that keeps changing color, and the aether engines that once held the weather have begun to think for themselves. In the Free Port of Maunder's Reach, a tide-engine has held back the drowned sea for three generations. Tonight it woke up, stopped holding, and started broadcasting in a language no living scholar can read — and the Assembly has sent one envoy down to the waterline to find out what it wants before the next surge.

-- The Waking Tide --
For three generations the tide-engine held the sea off Maunder's Reach without anyone once asking it to. Tonight, an hour after dusk, it stopped — and the water started climbing the seawall in the dark.
Then it began to broadcast: a signal on every aether-band at once, structured, patient, unmistakably speech, and in nothing anyone alive can read.
The Assembly sent one person down to the waterline. Engine-blooded, aether in the veins, able to stand where the signal is loudest and still think. The crowd on the wall parts to let you through and does not follow.
Somewhere below your feet, the thing that has kept this city dry is waiting to see who it sent.

You open yourself to the broadcast — not to translate it, not yet, but to find the SHAPE of it: is this a warning, a demand, or a question? Declare how you meet it.
